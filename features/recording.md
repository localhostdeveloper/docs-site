<span class="m-kicker">Made to be safe</span>
<h2>Recordings you can count on</h2>
</div>
<div class="m-grid m-grid-3">
<div class="m-card"><h3>Safe against a crash</h3><p>Files are written so that a crash or a power cut loses at most the last couple of seconds. Everything before plays.</p></div>
<div class="m-card"><h3>Never affects the stream</h3><p>A slow or full disk stops the recording, not the broadcast. Your viewers never notice a recording problem.</p></div>
<div class="m-card"><h3>Split for you</h3><p>A long stream becomes one file about every hour, cut at a clean point so each file plays on its own.</p></div>
<div class="m-card"><h3>Playable while recording</h3><p>Recordings are MPEG-TS files that play in VLC and most editors, even while they are still being written.</p></div>
<div class="m-card"><h3>Disk warnings</h3><p>The dashboard warns when the recordings disk is 85% full, and again at 95%.</p></div>
<div class="m-card"><h3>Clean-up when you want it</h3><p>Old recordings can be deleted after a number of days you choose. It is off by default: nothing is deleted unless you ask.</p></div>
