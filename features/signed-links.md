<h1>Only the people you invite can watch</h1>
<p class="m-lead">For ticketed events, members-only services and private meetings: a protected stream plays only from a signed link that you or your website made, and only until the time you choose. One link can be limited to a number of devices, so it can't simply be passed around.</p>

<span class="m-kicker">In the dashboard</span>
<h2>Protect a stream in one switch</h2>
<p>Open a stream's Playback tab, switch on "Require a signed link", choose how long new viewers can start watching, and make a link to send. Or protect every stream on the server at once.</p>
<ul class="m-checks">
<li>Covers every way of watching: the player page, HLS, DASH, RTMP and SRT</li>
<li>Links from 1 hour to 30 days</li>
<li>Someone who started in time carries on to the end of the event</li>
<li>Viewers with an old or altered link see a clear message</li>
</ul>

<div class="m-head">
<span class="m-kicker">For ticketed events</span>
<h2>Built to be connected to your website</h2>
<p>Unda does not take payments. Your website or ticketing system does, and gives each buyer a link that Unda checks.</p>
</div>
<div class="m-grid m-grid-3 m-steps">
<div class="m-step"><h3>A viewer buys a ticket</h3><p>On your website, through the payment provider you already use.</p></div>
<div class="m-step"><h3>Your website makes a link</h3><p>A few lines of code sign the stream name and an end time with your server's secret. Examples are in the guide.</p></div>
<div class="m-step"><h3>Unda checks it</h3><p>The link plays until its time is up. Without one, or after it, the stream is refused before any video is sent.</p></div>
</div>
</div>
</section>
<section class="m-section">
<div class="m-wrap">
<div class="m-specs">
<div class="m-spec"><h3>Devices per link</h3><ul><li>Limit a link to one device, or a number you choose</li><li>The newest device wins; the one pushed off gets a "Watch here" button to take it back</li><li>Per stream, or per link for tickets of different sizes</li></ul></div>
<div class="m-spec"><h3>Security</h3><ul><li>Each link is signed for one stream and can't be altered</li><li>Change the secret to cancel every link made with it</li><li>Who protected what, and who saw the secret, is in the audit log</li></ul></div>
</div>
