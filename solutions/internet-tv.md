<h2>Programmed in the dashboard, on air all day</h2>
<p>Upload programmes, plan the day with fixed times for the news and following items for the rest, and repeat it daily. Live shows take over when they start, and the schedule picks up when they end.</p>
<ul class="m-checks">
<li>Seamless changes between programmes, no player restarts</li>
<li>Live takeover in about 3 seconds</li>
<li>An as-run log of everything that aired, for your advertisers</li>
<li>Several channels on one server</li>
</ul>
