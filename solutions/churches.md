<h2>From the church to every screen</h2>
</div>
<div class="m-grid m-grid-3 m-steps">
<div class="m-step"><h3>At church</h3><p>Cameras into OBS, vMix or a hardware switcher, streaming to your Unda server over RTMP or SRT.</p></div>
<div class="m-step"><h3>On the server</h3><p>Unda records the service, makes qualities for mobile data, and sends it to Facebook and YouTube.</p></div>
<div class="m-step"><h3>Members watch</h3><p>On your website, on Facebook, on YouTube, on smart TVs, wherever they are.</p></div>
