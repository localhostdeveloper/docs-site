<h2>Who we are</h2>
<p>UndaMedia makes Unda Media Server. UndaMedia is a product of LocalCode Technology, a subsidiary of Richben Media. In this policy "we" and "us" mean UndaMedia. For anything about your information, email <a href="mailto:support@undamedia.com">support@undamedia.com</a>.</p>
<h2>This website</h2>
<h3>The contact form</h3>
<p>When you send the form we receive your name, email address, organisation (if you give one), the topic you chose and your message. We use them only to answer you and, if you become a customer, to keep a record of what we discussed. We don't add you to a mailing list, and we won't send you marketing unless you ask for it.</p>
<p>The form is delivered to our mailbox by an email service (Resend), and our mailbox is run by an email provider. They handle the message only to deliver and store it for us.</p>
<h3>Website logs</h3>
<p>This website is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, it records technical details of each visit, such as your IP address, browser and the page you asked for, to keep the site running and secure. We don't use these records to identify you.</p>
<h3>No cookies, no tracking</h3>
<p>This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. If you switch between light and dark mode, your browser remembers the choice on your own device; it is never sent to us.</p>
<p>Our Facebook and X pages, and any other site we link to, have their own privacy policies.</p>
<h2>Unda Media Server, the software</h2>
<h3>Your server, your data</h3>
<p>Unda runs on your server. Your streams, recordings, viewers, viewer statistics and team accounts stay there and never pass through us. You decide what is collected about your viewers and how long it is kept, so you are responsible for it towards them.</p>
<h3>What the software sends out</h3>
<ul>
<li><b>Update check.</b> Twice a day the server asks GitHub, where our releases are published, whether a newer version exists. The request carries only the Unda version number; GitHub sees your server's IP address, as with any download. You can switch the check off in the configuration file.</li>
<li><b>Your own alerts.</b> If you set up alert webhooks (for example to Slack), the server sends alerts to the addresses you chose, and nowhere else.</li>
<li><b>Your own destinations.</b> Restreaming, outputs and similar features send video only where you tell them to.</li>
</ul>
<p>The licence is a signed file checked on your server. There is no activation call and no usage reporting to us.</p>
<h3>Support access</h3>
<p>We can sign in to your server only when its owner gives us support access from the License page. That access ends by itself at the time the owner chose, or sooner if they remove it, and everything we do is in your audit log. We use what we see only to help with the problem you asked about.</p>
<h3>Managed hosting</h3>
<p>If we run the server for you, your data is on a server we manage on your behalf. We access it only to run, maintain and support it, and the details are in our agreement with you.</p>
<h2>Who we share information with</h2>
<p>We don't sell or rent your information. We share it only with the services that help us run this website and answer email (hosting, email delivery and our mailbox), and only as far as they need. We would also share it if the law requires us to.</p>
<p>These services may store information outside your country, including in the United States.</p>
<h2>How long we keep it</h2>
<p>We keep messages for as long as we need them to answer you and, for customers, as part of our business records. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time, unless we must keep them by law.</p>
<h2>Your choices</h2>
<p>You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email <a href="mailto:support@undamedia.com">support@undamedia.com</a> and we'll answer within 30 days. You can also complain to the data protection authority in your country.</p>
<h2>Changes</h2>
<p>If we change this policy we'll update it here and change the date at the top.</p>
