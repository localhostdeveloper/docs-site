<span class="m-kicker">Why UndaMedia</span>
<h2>Your server, your viewers, your rules</h2>
<p>Platforms decide who sees your stream, put their adverts on it and keep the figures. With Unda the whole service runs on a server you choose, in your country or in the cloud.</p>
<ul class="m-checks">
<li>No limit on viewers in any package: your bandwidth is the only limit</li>
<li>Viewers watch on your website, under your name</li>
<li>Installed with one command, or as a Docker image, with automatic HTTPS</li>
<li>Nothing passes through us: no account with us, no hidden access</li>
</ul>

<span class="m-kicker">Getting started</span>
<h2>On air in three steps</h2>
</div>
<div class="m-grid m-grid-3 m-steps">
<div class="m-step"><h3>Install it</h3><p>One command on an Ubuntu or Debian server with a domain name, or the Docker image. It gets its own HTTPS certificate.</p></div>
<div class="m-step"><h3>Create a channel</h3><p>Open the dashboard, create your account and a channel. You get a public name to watch by and a secret key to stream with.</p></div>
<div class="m-step"><h3>Go live</h3><p>Point OBS or your encoder at the server, share your player page, and add YouTube or Facebook if you like.</p></div>
