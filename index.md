<h2>What Unda does</h2>
<p>It takes streams in from encoders, cameras and broadcast equipment, makes the qualities each screen needs, records them, and sends them on to platforms and TV equipment.</p>

<h2>No platform between you and your viewers</h2>
<p>On a platform, someone else decides who sees the stream, puts adverts on it and keeps the figures. Unda runs on a server you choose, in your country or in the cloud, and the viewers and the figures stay with you.</p>
<ul class="m-checks">
<li>No limit on viewers in any package: your bandwidth is the only limit</li>
<li>Viewers watch on your website, under your name</li>
<li>Installed with one command on Linux or Windows 11, or from the Docker image</li>
<li>Your streams and viewers never pass through us, and we can sign in only when you allow it</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2>One dashboard for presenters, operators and clients</h2>
<p>Each presenter, operator and client signs in with their own account and sees only the streams and buttons their role allows. Alerts tell you when a stream drops, goes silent or freezes.</p>
<ul class="m-checks">
<li>Roles from owner to streamer, with two-factor sign-in</li>
<li>Stream health, alerts and an event log that say what is wrong</li>
<li>Who is watching, from where, for how long, downloadable for reports</li>
<li>Light or dark, on a laptop or a phone</li>
</ul>

<h2>Getting started</h2>
</div>
<div class="m-grid m-grid-3 m-steps">
<div class="m-step"><h3>Install it</h3><p>One command on a Linux server or a Windows 11 PC, or the Docker image. With a domain name it gets its own HTTPS certificate.</p></div>
<div class="m-step"><h3>Create a channel</h3><p>Open the dashboard, create your account and a channel. You get a public name to watch by and a secret key to stream with.</p></div>
<div class="m-step"><h3>Go live</h3><p>Point OBS or your encoder at the server, share your player page, and add YouTube or Facebook if you like.</p></div>
