<h2>Using the logo</h2>
<ul>
<li>Use the files as they are: please don't stretch, recolour, rotate or add effects to them.</li>
<li>Leave clear space around the logo, at least half the height of the symbol.</li>
<li>Use the white version on dark or busy backgrounds.</li>
<li>Write the company as one word, <b>UndaMedia</b>, and the product as <b>Unda Media Server</b>.</li>
</ul>
<h2>About UndaMedia</h2>
<p>UndaMedia makes Unda Media Server, live streaming and broadcast software that TV channels and broadcasters run on their own servers. It takes live video in over SRT, RTMP, WebRTC and other protocols, sends it out to viewers, social platforms, TV equipment and satellite teleports, and runs 24/7 channels, backup sources, recording and monitoring from one dashboard.</p>
<p>Unda means “wave” in Latin, the waves that carry radio and television, and “to build” in Swahili. UndaMedia is a product of LocalCode Technology.</p>
