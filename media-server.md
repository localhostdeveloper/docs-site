<span class="m-kicker">Capacity</span>
<h2>Light on the server, so your bandwidth is the limit</h2>
<p>Passing streams through costs very little. What we measured on a test machine:</p>
<ul class="m-checks">
<li>10 incoming 720p streams with 25 viewers: about a quarter of one CPU core</li>
<li>200 viewers of a 1080p stream (about 1 Gb/s): about a quarter of one core</li>
<li>24 hours unattended on a cloud server, with no restart and flat memory</li>
</ul>
<p>Making extra qualities is the expensive part: plan about one CPU core per stream with adaptive bitrate, or use a GPU.</p>
</div>

<div class="m-head">
<span class="m-kicker">Technical specifications</span>
<h2>The details</h2>
</div>
<div class="m-specs">
<div class="m-spec"><h3>Live input</h3><ul><li>RTMP, RTMPS, SRT (caller and listener, AES encryption), UDP and RTP (unicast, multicast), RIST Simple Profile</li><li>H.264 video with AAC passed through untouched</li><li>MP2 and AC-3 audio converted to AAC; MPEG-2 video converted to H.264 on multi-channel inputs</li><li>Packet loss, continuity and TR 101 290 checks on transport streams</li></ul></div>
<div class="m-spec"><h3>Delivery</h3><ul><li>HLS with a built-in player and quality menu; MPEG-DASH</li><li>RTMP and SRT playback; GOP cache for a fast start</li><li>Restreaming over RTMP, RTMPS, SRT, UDP, RTP and RIST, each destination independent</li><li>Broadcast outputs: 576p, 720p50 or 1080i25, H.264, HEVC or MPEG-2, constant bitrate</li><li>Multi-channel transport streams with DVB channel names and now/next guide</li></ul></div>
<div class="m-spec"><h3>Adaptive bitrate</h3><ul><li>Up to 8 qualities, 1080p down to 144p by default, with aligned keyframes</li><li>Planned per source: size, shape, frame rate and bitrate</li><li>On the CPU; NVIDIA, Intel and AMD GPU encoders through FFmpeg (ask us about your card)</li></ul></div>
<div class="m-spec"><h3>Recording and 24/7 channels</h3><ul><li>Recording to MPEG-TS files, split hourly, safe against a crash, optional clean-up</li><li>Media library with resumable uploads</li><li>Schedules with fixed and following items, daily repeat, live takeover, as-run log</li></ul></div>
<div class="m-spec"><h3>Reliability</h3><ul><li>Backup encoders and other streams as backup sources, with automatic return</li><li>A slow viewer or destination never holds up the others</li><li>Alerts on the dashboard and by webhook</li></ul></div>
<div class="m-spec"><h3>Access and security</h3><ul><li>Accounts with roles: owner, admin, operator, streamer</li><li>Two-factor sign-in, invitations, API keys, audit log</li><li>Signed playback links with device limits</li><li>Automatic HTTPS (Let's Encrypt) or your own certificate</li></ul></div>
<div class="m-spec"><h3>Management</h3><ul><li>Web dashboard, light or dark, on desktop and phone</li><li>REST API for everything the dashboard does</li><li>Prometheus metrics and an hour of history in the dashboard</li></ul></div>
<div class="m-spec"><h3>Running it</h3><ul><li>One program for Linux on Intel/AMD and ARM; Docker image</li><li>FFmpeg for adaptive bitrate, 24/7 channels and conversions</li><li>Database backed up automatically before every upgrade</li></ul></div>
